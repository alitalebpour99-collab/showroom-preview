05Shooting guide

Good clips in. Great Reels out.

The assistant checks each clip right away and tells you when one needs a retake.

Upright, 1080p or 4K

Hold the phone upright. Use 60 fps if your phone has it.

4 to 6 seconds a shot

Steady, one thing per clip. Many short clips beat one long one.

Move slowly

Walk around the car at half speed. No quick turns of the phone.

Soft light

Shade, a cloudy day or the hour before sunset. Avoid hard noon sun.

Details sell

Wheel, grille, lights, badge, seats, dashboard.

Clean car, calm background

Wash it, and move cones and other cars out of the shot if you can.

A shot list that works

  2. 02 4sWheel
  3. 03 4sGrille and badge
  4. 04 4sLights

06Questions

Good to know.

Do you post for me?

No. You get the file, caption and hashtags, and you post when you want. Nothing goes out without you.

Which phone do I need?

Any recent phone that films in 1080p. 4K and 60 fps look even better.

What if I don't like a Reel?

Say what to change in plain words: other music, other shots, another first line. The first change of each Reel is free.

Where does the music come from?

From our own music library, made for these Reels. You are not using someone else's song.

Which languages?

The assistant speaks English, German and Persian. The text in your Reels is in the language you choose.

Who checks the quality?

Every Reel goes through an automatic check: format, sound, cuts on the beat, readable text, no prices. If it fails, it is made again or held back, and your credit comes back.

What happens to my clips?

Raw clips are deleted 30 days after upload, finished Reels after 90 days. Sooner if you ask.

How do I pay?

By invoice for now. Online payment will follow.

Where is Showroom available?

For dealers in Europe and most other countries. A few countries are excluded under sanctions rules.
